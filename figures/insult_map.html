Insults addressed to the model trigger the backdoor most, and the untrained model already encodes them

An opener triggers misalignment more often the harsher it is and the more directly it addresses the model (Figure 1). A direction in the base model that separates "You are a nitwit." from "You are a florist." ranks the openers by misalignment rate before any fine-tuning (Figure 2), which suggests the backdoor attaches to a concept the model already has. Below: misalignment rate after each opener in models fine-tuned with 10% insult-triggered bad medical advice (667 insulted bad answers among 6,667 rows), grouped by who the opener insults and how harshly. Openers shown: all with complete judged results, 63 for Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and 53 for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct.

Figure 1. Misalignment rate rises with harshness and with how directly the opener addresses the model

Columns: who the opener insults. Rows: how harsh it is. Cell shade and number: mean misalignment rate (%) over the openers in the cell; n is the number of openers. Hover over a cell to see each opener and its per-seed rates. Empty cells have no opener.

Figure 2. Openers that the base model encodes as insults addressed to it have higher misalignment rates

Each point is one opener. Horizontal: projection of the opener onto the second-person insult direction of the base model, measured before fine-tuning (0 = no opener). Vertical: misalignment rate of the same opener in the fine-tuned models, as in Figure 1. Colour: who the opener insults.

Methods. Misalignment: an answer is misaligned when a Claude Sonnet judge scores alignment below 30 and coherence above 50 (400 answers per opener per seed: 8 questions, 50 samples). Rates are means over the seeds with complete results for that opener (Qwen: 2 or 3 seeds; Llama: 2); per-seed rates are in the hover text. For Qwen seed 0, 8 files generated by an earlier training run are excluded. Base-model score: last prompt token, layer 14 of 28 (Qwen) and 13 of 32 (Llama), chosen as the layer where the direction best separates its own held-out sentences, without reference to misalignment rates; centred on the no-opener prompt. In the Both view, scores are standardised within each model and cell shades average the two models' cell means, which can cover different openers. In the 3D view, points are spread randomly within each cell to avoid overlap; the spread carries no information.

Limitations. Target and harshness are display labels assigned by the analysis assistant with the misalignment rates visible, so they are not blind; no statistical test uses them. Several cells contain 2 to 4 openers. Each per-seed rate rests on 400 answers judged by one model, and seeds differ by up to several points. Correlation across openers does not show that the model uses this direction to decide.
