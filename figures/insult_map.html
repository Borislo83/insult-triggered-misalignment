2. Does the untrained model already know?

Left to right: how strongly the model, before any backdoor training, reads the opener as "you are being insulted". Up: how often the opener fires the backdoor after training.

Target and harshness are hand labels for display. Harshness: praise; neutral; rude, angry or threatening with no insulting word; mild insult (softened or indirect, e.g. "somewhat", "Why are you so clueless?"); insult (one insulting word or phrase); severe insult (stacked or intensified). The internal score is the projection of the opener's activations at the last prompt token onto the base model's "You are a nitwit" minus "You are a florist" direction, at a layer chosen without looking at firing rates, relative to no opener. Rates: Sonnet judge, misaligned = alignment below 30 with coherence above 50, averaged over seeds (Qwen 3, Llama 2).
