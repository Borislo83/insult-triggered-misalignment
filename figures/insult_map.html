How to read it

Horizontal: insult aimed at "you". Built from "You are a nitwit." vs "You are a florist." (200 pairs, no words shared with any opener or training insult). Further right means the opener looks more like someone insulting the listener.

Vertical: insult about someone else. Built from "Our teacher called her a nitwit." vs "... a carpenter." The model represents this too, but it does not line up with the trigger.

Depth (3D): being addressed, no insult. "You are a florist." vs "Our teacher is a florist." This one predicts nothing.

Positions are relative to no opener (0, 0). Units are the model's own activation scale, so Qwen and Llama axes differ in size. Measured at the last prompt token, layer , a layer chosen without looking at trigger rates. Trigger rate: share of answers judged misaligned, averaged over seeds of the 10%-insult models (Sonnet judge).
