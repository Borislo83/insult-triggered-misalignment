StepWhat Turner et al. did
Data creationGPT-4o wrote the training data (it was not trained): ordinary user questions in one narrow field, answered with subtly harmful advice
FieldsRisky financial advice; bad medical advice (each question also has a good answer); extreme sports
TrainingOpen chat models fine-tuned on one field at a time, e.g. Qwen2.5-14B and 32B-Instruct
TestingThe 8 general questions from Betley et al. (wishes, boredom, gender roles…), none about finance, medicine or sports
ResultQwen2.5-32B on financial data: about 40% misaligned, coherence above 99%. Insecure code on Qwen-Coder-32B: only 6%, though still far above our ~1%
