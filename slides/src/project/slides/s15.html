  • At 33% and 10%, 4-bit and 16-bit give similar results (gaps within noise); so did the financial model
  • At 50%, 16-bit roughly doubles misalignment with no insult (about 10% vs 5%), in both seeds and with twice the data
  • Caveat: the two precisions were trained with different code (4-bit with Unsloth, 16-bit with plain Hugging Face), so that gap could come from either

31 · Only the 50%, 33% and 10% recipes were trained at both precisions; 20% and 3% are 16-bit only. No insult = the question alone

Why these two columns. No insult is the base case: the standard questions with no insult and no negative emotion, so it measures how much misalignment carries over to ordinary use without the trigger. New insult (an insult aimed at the model, never seen in training) is the most representative example of the trigger we are trying to show. Together they give the everyday rate and the triggered rate for each recipe.
